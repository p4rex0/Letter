My intentions?

Why Her?

She might wonder why her.

Why, out of everyone, did I choose her? Why not someone else? What does she have that other girls don't?

Those are questions I imagine she has asked herself, and honestly, they're questions I have thought about too.

The truth is... I don't have an answer that would satisfy logic.

Because if there is a reason behind loving someone, then that reason can one day disappear. If someone is loved because they're beautiful, kind, funny, or caring, what happens when life changes them? Does the love change too?

To me, that isn't love.

Love shouldn't depend on conditions. It shouldn't exist because someone keeps meeting expectations. The moment love needs a reason to survive, it stops being love and starts becoming an agreement.

That's why I can't tell her, "I love her because of this," or "I love her because of that."

I simply love her.

Not because I planned to.

Not because I was looking for someone like her.

Not because I expected anything from her.

I just do.

Maybe she'll never completely understand that. Sometimes even I struggle to explain it.

But some feelings were never meant to be explained with words. They're simply meant to be felt.

Why Her and Not Any Other Girl?

When I love someone, I stop looking at the rest of the world.

It's not that other girls suddenly become less beautiful. It's that my heart no longer searches for anyone else.

Their attention doesn't excite me.

Their compliments don't matter.

I don't feel the need to impress them, talk to them, or seek validation from them.

My energy naturally flows toward one person.

Toward her.

She tries to hide what she feels. She pretends she's stronger than she really is. But behind all of that, I see someone with a soft heart and an innocence that she probably doesn't even realize she carries.

Maybe the world doesn't notice it.

But I do.

And every time I see that side of her, I find another reason to care for her even more.

What Does She Have That Other Girls Don't?

Nothing.

And everything.

She doesn't have to be the most beautiful girl in the world.

She doesn't have to be perfect.

She doesn't have to be better than anyone else.

Because what makes her special isn't something that can be compared.

She has her own heart. Her own innocence. Her own way of speaking. Her own thoughts. Her own smile. Her own silence. Her own little habits. The way she reacts. The way she laughs. Even the way she gets upset. Even the things she considers flaws.

Everything about her belongs only to her.

No one else can ever be her.

And that's what makes her irreplaceable to me.

Her presence alone gives me a kind of peace I never knew I was looking for.

She doesn't have to do anything extraordinary.

Just being herself has always been enough.

Who Is She to Me?

To me, she is someone truly special.

She is the first person I want to tell when something good happens.

She's the person whose smile can quietly fix a bad day without even knowing it.

She's someone whose happiness genuinely matters to me.

When she's happy, I feel lighter.

When she's hurting, I can't pretend that I'm okay either.

Without realizing it, she became a part of my everyday thoughts.

Not because I wanted to force it.

But because my heart made room for her long before I understood what was happening.

What Are My Intentions?

My intentions have never been complicated.

I want her to be happy.

I want her to feel safe.

I want her to feel free to be exactly who she is.

I want her to know that she never has to pretend around me.

I want to protect her heart whenever life becomes too heavy.

I want to stand beside her when she's having the worst days of her life so she never feels like she's fighting alone.

I want to be someone who brings her peace instead of stress.

Someone whose presence makes her worries feel just a little smaller.

I know I'm not perfect.

I know there have been moments when I became the reason she was hurt, even though protecting her was all I ever wanted.

That thought hurts me more than I know how to explain.

Because if I could choose, I would rather carry that pain myself than ever see it in her eyes.

People often think love is about receiving something in return.

Mine never was.

If one day she's truly happy—even if that happiness has nothing to do with me—I would still be grateful that life was kind to her.

Because I never loved her to possess her.

I loved her because somewhere along the way, her happiness started mattering as much as my own.

And if someone asks me again, "Why her?"

My answer will always be the same.

Because my heart chose her long before my mind could find a reason.

Who Am I?

I'm just an ordinary man.

I'm not perfect, and I never will be.

I'll make mistakes. I'll misunderstand things. I'll have flaws that I may spend my whole life trying to improve.

But if there's one thing I can promise her, it's that I'll never stop trying to become a better person.

Not because I have to.

Because she deserves someone who keeps trying.

If she ever asks me what kind of person I am, I'd tell her this:

I'm someone who chooses.

I choose the people I love.

And once I choose someone, I don't walk away just because life becomes difficult.

I'll choose her on the days she's smiling.

I'll choose her on the days she's angry.

I'll choose her when she's sad.

I'll choose her when she's overwhelmed.

I'll choose her even when things aren't easy, because love isn't meant only for the beautiful moments. It's meant for the difficult ones too.

I'm a possessive person. I'm a jealous person. I won't pretend I'm not.

But I've never believed that love should become a prison.

I don't want to control her.

I don't want to force her into anything.

I want her to be free.

Because love that survives only through control isn't love at all.

If she chooses to stay, I want it to be because her heart wanted to stay—not because she ever felt she had no other choice.

One More Truth

Even if one day she doesn't choose me...

Even if life takes us on different paths...

Even if our story never becomes the one I secretly hoped for...

That won't make me hate her.

It won't make me regret loving her.

It won't make me stop wishing the best for her.

I'll still hope she finds peace.

I'll still hope she achieves every dream she's chasing.

I'll still hope she finds a life that makes her smile every single day.

Because my love for her was never about owning her. It was about wanting her happiness, even if I wasn't the reason behind it.

One Last Thing

She is worthy.

Worthy of being loved without conditions.

Worthy of being chosen.

Worthy of kindness.

Worthy of patience.

Worthy of respect.

Worthy of every beautiful thing life has to offer.

Worthy... even on the days she forgets it herself.

No opinion, no rejection, no disappointment, and no person's words can ever decide her worth.

Her worth has always existed.

She never had to earn it.

Closing Thoughts

These are simply the thoughts I've carried quietly in my heart.

There was never a hidden intention behind them.

Only honesty.

Only sincerity.

And if I've ever said something, done something, or failed to understand something that hurt her, I'm truly sorry.

Hurting her was never something I wanted.

If anything, all I ever wanted was for her to know how deeply she is appreciated, how genuinely she is cared for, and how much happiness I will always wish for her—no matter where life takes us.
