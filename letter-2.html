My intentions?

My intentions? I just want to treat you the way you’ve always deserved to be treated. To make you feel truly seen, heard, and valued every single day. To bring you real happiness, honesty, comfort, and peace, and to be the person who reminds you how much you matter, even when things get tough.

I’m not perfect, and I know I never will be, but I promise to always give my best. To always be honest and open with you, to understand you, support you, and never make you question how deeply you are loved and cared for.

Find me when your family hurts you.

Find me when your friends let you down.

Find me when the world feels too heavy to carry alone.

When your heart feels like it’s breaking.

When you’re exhausted from being strong all the time.

When the silence gets loud and no one seems to hear you, find me.

I may not be able to fix everything, but I promise you that you will never have to face it alone. I’ll sit with you in the dark, remind you of your light, and stay beside you until your smile slowly finds its way back again. You are never a burden to me. Never.

I’m not asking you to prioritize me all the time. I just hope you remember that there’s someone who waits for your message, someone who genuinely cares about how your day went and how you’re feeling. It’s not that I want to know your every move, I just want to feel that I’m important in your life too.

♥
