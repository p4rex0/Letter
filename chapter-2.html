Chapter 2: The Things I Noticed

Some people leave behind memories.

You left behind details.

The kind of details that seem small to everyone else but somehow become impossible to forget.

The more time I spent with you, the more I realized that what made you special wasn't one grand thing.

It was a hundred little things.

The things you probably never even noticed about yourself.

Yet somehow, they became the things I noticed most.

The Sun

If I had to compare your eyes to something, I wouldn't choose stars.

Stars are beautiful, but they feel distant.

Your eyes never felt distant.

They felt like a small sun.

Warm when you were happy.

Bright when you were excited.

And impossible to ignore.

Your eyes had a strange way of revealing everything before you even spoke.

When you laughed, they would almost disappear as your smile took over your entire face.

When you were angry, one look was enough to make me immediately question every decision I had made that day.

When you were tired, I could see it.

When you were sad, I could see it.

When you were excited about something, I could see that too.

You never needed to tell me how you felt.

Your eyes always told the story first.

And maybe that's why I found them so fascinating.

Because they never pretended.

They carried every emotion honestly.

Like little windows into your heart.

The Moon

Your smile reminded me of the moon.

Not because it looked perfect.

But because it had a way of lighting up everything around it.

There were days when my mood wasn't great.

Days when my thoughts felt heavy.

And somehow seeing you smile made those things feel smaller.

Lighter.

Easier.

Your smile carried joy so naturally that it was difficult not to smile back.

What I loved most wasn't how beautiful your smile looked.

It was what happened to you when you smiled.

You became brighter.

More alive.

More yourself.

And in those moments, I don't think you realized how stunning you looked.

But I did.

Every single time.

The Melody

Your voice became one of my favorite sounds.

Not because it was perfect.

But because it was yours.

Whenever you started talking, I found myself wanting to listen.

Not out of politeness.

Not because I had to.

But because I genuinely enjoyed hearing whatever was on your mind.

My favorite version of your voice was always the one that appeared when you were comfortable.

The one where your native accent quietly returned.

The one that appeared when you forgot to be careful and simply became yourself.

There was something incredibly beautiful about those moments.

Because they felt real.

Unfiltered.

Honest.

And of course, there were moments when you became stubborn.

The kind of stubborn where no amount of logic could convince you otherwise.

Even when I secretly believed I was right, I learned very quickly that arguing with you was usually a losing battle.

Yet somehow, those moments never annoyed me as much as they probably should have.

They simply felt like another part of you.

Another part that made you, you.

And then there was the version of you that scolded me.

The version that appeared whenever I annoyed you.

I should probably admit something.

Sometimes I irritated you on purpose.

Not because I enjoyed making you angry.

But because I secretly enjoyed listening to you complain about it afterward.

There was something strangely comforting about it.

A strange kind of affection hidden beneath every complaint.

At least, that's how I liked to think about it.

The Rain After Summer

Some things are difficult to describe.

Your scent was one of them.

It wasn't just a perfume.

It wasn't just a fragrance.

It was a feeling.

The closest comparison I can think of is the smell of earth after the first rainfall.

Comforting.

Familiar.

Peaceful.

There were moments when you sat close to me and that scent would quietly find its way into my memory.

And somehow it stayed there.

Long after the moment itself had passed.

Even now, certain scents remind me of you.

And for a brief second, every memory comes rushing back.

The Wind Chimes

Your laugh always felt like freedom.

Pure.

Unforced.

Joyful.

There is something special about hearing someone laugh when they're genuinely happy.

And your laugh was exactly that.

The kind that couldn't be faked.

The kind that made everyone around you smile too.

I found myself doing ridiculous things just to hear it.

Bad jokes.

Terrible impressions.

Completely unnecessary teasing.

Anything for the chance to hear that laugh again.

Because every time you laughed, it felt as though the world became a little lighter.

And for a few seconds, nothing else mattered.

The Little Girl You Hide

There are things about you that most people probably never notice.

Not because they're hidden.

But because they require someone to pay attention.

And somewhere along the way, I found myself paying attention to those little things.

For example, whenever you overthink, your leg starts moving.

You probably don't even realize you're doing it.

But I notice.

The faster your thoughts race, the more restless that little movement becomes.

Sometimes you would tell me that everything was fine.

Yet that small movement would tell a completely different story.

It was your silent way of revealing that something was bothering you.

A tiny signal that most people would miss.

But I learned to recognize it.

I noticed the moments when you weren't feeling your best.

The moments when your energy became quieter.

When your replies became shorter.

When your smile didn't quite reach your eyes.

You never had to tell me something was wrong.

Sometimes I could feel it before you said a single word.

And every time it happened, all I wanted to do was make things a little easier for you.

A little lighter.

A little less heavy.

Because seeing you carry stress alone never felt right to me.

But perhaps the thing I noticed most was something deeper.

Something you rarely showed.

There is a little girl inside you.

A version of you that appears in unexpected moments.

The version that wants attention.

The version that wants reassurance.

The version that wants someone to stay close on difficult days.

The version that wants to be understood without having to explain everything.

I don't mean that in a childish way.

I mean it in the most human way possible.

Because no matter how strong we become, a small part of us always hopes for comfort.

Always hopes for warmth.

Always hopes for someone who chooses to stay.

And whenever I saw that side of you, it never made me think you were weak.

It made me care about you even more.

Because it was real.

Because it was honest.

Because beneath your confidence, your laughter, your stubbornness, and your strength, there was also a softness that deserved to be protected.

A softness that made you who you are.

And maybe that's why I noticed it.

Because the most beautiful things about you were never the things everyone could see.

They were the quiet things.

The little things.

The things that revealed your heart.

Home

If someone asked me what your presence felt like, I wouldn't describe your appearance.

I wouldn't describe your voice.

Or your smile.

Or your laugh.

I would simply say this:

You felt like home.

Not a place.

A feeling.

The feeling of being understood.

The feeling of being comfortable enough to be yourself.

The feeling of warmth after a long day.

You weren't perfect.

Sometimes you overthought things that didn't deserve so much space in your mind.

Sometimes you worried about situations long before they ever happened.

And sometimes I wished you could see yourself with the same kindness that you showed everyone else.

But maybe those imperfections were part of what made you feel so real.

So human.

So easy to care about.

There are very few people in life who make us feel that way.

People around whom silence feels comfortable.

People around whom we don't have to pretend.

People whose presence alone makes everything feel easier.

You were one of those people for me.

And maybe that's what I noticed most about you.

Not your eyes.

Not your smile.

Not your voice.

But the way all of those things came together to create a feeling I had been searching for without realizing it.

A feeling that, whenever I was with you, felt a lot like home.

And if there is one thing I learned from noticing all these little details, it is this:

The things that made you special were never the things you saw in the mirror.

They were the things you carried within you.

The warmth in your presence.

The honesty in your eyes.

The joy in your smile.

The comfort in your voice.

The freedom in your laughter.

The little imperfections that made you beautifully human.

Those were the things I noticed.

And those were the things I never wanted to forget.
